PgPee Support

Last updated: 30 August 2026 PgPee is an OpenPGP key manager and message encryptor for iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, and no company helpdesk — just an indie app that keeps your keys on your device.

How to get help

Email pgpeesupport@gmail.com. That is the only support channel. There is no phone number, no chat, and no promised response time. Mail is read, and replies go out as soon as reasonably possible.

A short note is enough: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and your iOS version. A screenshot helps when something looks wrong on screen. Please do not send private keys or passphrases.

What PgPee is

PgPee generates, stores, and uses OpenPGP keys on your iPhone. Encryption, decryption, signing, and key generation all happen on the device. The app makes no network requests of its own, so there is nothing to log in to and nothing of yours sitting on a backend.

Privacy

PgPee does not collect personal information. The full policy is at https://pgpeesupport.github.io/pgpee/.
