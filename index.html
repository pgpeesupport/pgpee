PgPee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026 PgPee is an OpenPGP key manager and message encryptor for iPhone. It is built so that there is nothing to collect: the app has no servers, no accounts, and makes no network requests of any kind.

What PgPee collects

Nothing. PgPee does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. No data ever leaves your device by way of this app.

What PgPee stores on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone:

  • Your private keys, in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the operating system and readable only while your device is unlocked.
  • Your public keys, contacts and their public keys, and key labels such as names and email addresses you enter or import, in the app's own local storage.
  • Your app settings, such as how soon the app re-locks.

Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also erase everything from within the app, under Settings → Erase All Data.

Network access

PgPee makes no network connections. Encryption, decryption, signing and key generation all happen on your device. Your messages and keys are never uploaded anywhere. The only exception is that tapping a link in Settings, such as this policy or the support page, hands the address to your browser. The app sends nothing with it.

Face ID and Touch ID

PgPee asks iOS to confirm it is you before unlocking. That check is performed by the operating system; the app is told only whether it succeeded and never receives your biometric data.

Sharing and the clipboard

When you copy a key or a message, or use the share sheet to export a key, that content goes wherever you send it. Those actions are always started by you, and what happens to the data afterwards is governed by whichever app or service you choose to send it to.

Children

PgPee is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
